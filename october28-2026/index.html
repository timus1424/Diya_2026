A Small Message for You, Ms. Mallick

Some people make the world a little more beautiful simply by being themselves. Today is a little reminder that the world is lucky to have you in it.

As you turn this page and step into another year, may you be greeted with quiet mornings, long cozy afternoons with your favourite stories, and moments that make you smile without even realizing it. You carry a unique warmth, and genuine kindness that brightens every room you walk into (always living upto your name eh...✨).

May this upcoming chapter bring you new adventures, unexpected discoveries, endless laughter, soft toy victories, and all the peaceful coffee breaks your heart desires. Never lose the wonder with which you look at the world, and remember to celebrate how wonderful you truly are (yes you very much so!)

May you get all your wishes except one, so you'll have something to strive for. And may the saddest day of your future be no worse than the happiest day of your past.
